# Brand Identity: FolioWrite (`replica-brand`)

## 1. Name Selection
Chosen name: **FolioWrite** (โฟลิโอไรท์)
- Evokes crisp printed folios and structured writing without sounding, looking, or meaning anything confusingly similar to the original target's trademark.
- Short, easy to pronounce in both Thai and English, clean for internal/team deployment or future standalone domain registration.

## 2. Palette
- Primary brand hue: **Deep Forest Teal (`#0d6b5e`)** on **Warm Paper Surface (`#f4f5f4` / `#e7eae8`)** — deliberately distinct from cobalt/royal blue office suites.
- Contrast checked via `contrast.py`: **16 pairs, 0 failing AA** (body text achieves 17.68:1 AAA).

## 3. Logo Brief
- **Concept**: A geometric folded paper sheet corner paired with two crisp horizontal text lines inside a rounded square (`#0d6b5e` fill, `#ffffff` strokes).
- **Mark type**: Symbol + clean geometric wordmark (`FolioWrite`).
- **Scale**: Crisp at 16x16px favicon, 192x192px & 512x512px PWA home-screen icons.

## 4. Brand Voice
- **Calm, not sterile**: Keeps tools organized without shouting or nagging with cloud upsells.
- **Bilingual-friendly, not awkward**: Clear Thai & English labels tailored for everyday Thai/international office documents.
- **Local-first, not complicated**: Emphasizes instant saving on the user's device and effortless `.docx` / PDF portability.
